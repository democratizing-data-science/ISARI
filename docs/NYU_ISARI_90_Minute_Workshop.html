Central argument

For academic research, the most powerful AI is not always the most valuable AI.

A smaller, local, deliberately bounded model can be methodologically preferable when it generates possibilities, surfaces comparisons, and returns the researcher to the evidence instead of delivering a polished product that risks displacing scholarly contribution.

Condensed answer: less model power can create more researcher participation. The productive endpoint is not a model-written answer. It is a better informed researcher-authored answer.

Use critically

Epistemological spine

Truly Equal Status Design: outputs are maps, not meanings.

Quantitative, computational, visual, and generative outputs can clarify where to look and what to compare. They remain incomplete until the researcher returns to the original qualitative evidence and constructs a contextualized interpretation.

The recursive TESD movement

Original qualitative evidence
→
Computational or visual structure
→
Patterns, contrasts, anomalies
→
Return to evidence
→
Researcher interpretation
Key rule: a generated statement becomes scholarly material only after it is checked against recordings, transcripts, quotations, figures, fieldnotes, or other original evidence.

How ISARI operationalizes TESD

  • Machine transcription is treated as provisional evidence.
  • Transcript Review supports wording correction, speaker confirmation, Split, Merge, notes, and review status.
  • Evidence scope controls make inclusion decisions explicit.
  • Exploratory and formal pathways distinguish analytical purposes.
  • Provenance and safeguards remain visible with results.
  • Stale-result detection identifies interpretations that no longer match current evidence.
  • Result versions preserve analytical evolution rather than silently replacing it.

Researcher authority

ISARI can organize materials, identify contrasts, suggest possible interpretations, translate visual structures into prose, and identify unresolved questions.

The researcher still decides: what the evidence means, whether a claim is adequate, which interpretation is retained, how uncertainty is represented, and what ultimately enters the scholarly record.

A concrete example: correcting the analytical unit

A machine-generated transcript turn may contain more than one speaker or fragment one contribution across several turns. If that structure is accepted uncritically, later coding and interpretation may misrepresent who said what. ISARI allows the researcher to listen to the source recording, revise wording, split or merge turns, confirm speaker roles, add notes, and save the reviewed structure before analysis. The interface feature is therefore a methodological safeguard, not merely an editing convenience.

Privacy by architecture

Evidence, interpretation, and responsibility remain together.

Local and fully offline operation reduces unnecessary external transfer of sensitive evidence. It extends data stewardship beyond storage into transcription, interpretation, visual analysis, memoing, and writing.

Exports & recovery

No required cloud-AI processing, API key, online account, or remote model call for the core workflow.

1. Data custody

Core materials remain on the researcher's computer. Locality makes processing location part of the study's governance.

2. Evidence integrity

Machine outputs remain reviewable. Structural transcript edits, evidence scope, save states, and source links make changes inspectable.

3. Interpretive accountability

AI outputs are brainstorming artifacts. Provenance, constraints, review notes, and result versions maintain a visible path from evidence to claim.

4. Continuity and recovery

Dirty-state warnings, project persistence, recovery drafts, and stale-result detection protect the intellectual history of a project.

5. Inclusion

Offline, no-code tooling reduces dependence on continuous connectivity, commercial subscriptions, programming expertise, and remote infrastructure.

6. Multimodal stewardship

Charts, screenshots, network maps, temporal frames, and geospatial displays can remain sensitive. Local visual interpretation closes a gap left by text-only privacy.

Local does not automatically mean ethical

Local operation does not replace informed consent, secure devices, access controls, de-identification, retention and deletion plans, responsible quotation practices, or IRB review. Relationships, sequences, geographies, visual structures, and combinations of attributes can remain identifying even after names are removed.
IRB and research-governance conversation starters
  • Where will source data, prompts, outputs, versions, and recovery copies be stored?
  • Will any material leave the local machine at any analytical stage?
  • Which textual, relational, temporal, visual, or spatial materials remain indirectly identifying?
  • Who can access recordings, reviewed transcripts, and analytical histories?
  • How will machine suggestions be distinguished from researcher findings?
  • How will exported projects and backups be retained, encrypted, shared, or destroyed?
63-minute live demonstration

ISARI is a local research environment, not merely a chat interface.

The demonstration follows project continuity from source evidence through review, analysis, provenance-aware results, and evidence-grounded synthesis.

Demo principle: do not demonstrate every button. Demonstrate the decisions that preserve researcher contribution: what enters the project, how evidence is corrected, what the model may do, how claims are checked, and how the analytical trail is retained.

What ISARI does not claim to do

  • Replace the researcher or determine final meaning.
  • Turn generated prose into a verified finding.
  • Guarantee transcription, speaker identity, OCR, or visual interpretation accuracy.
  • Make weak evidence methodologically strong.
  • Remove source-data bias.
  • Replace consent, IRB review, confidentiality, or secure device management.
  • Replace specialized frameworks such as LACOID, MDCOR, SENA, NAQD, GRATIS, VERITAS, or GeoStoryTelling.

Suggested bounded prompt for the demonstration

Using only the reviewed evidence selected for this analysis, identify two plausible contrasts and three questions I should investigate. For every observation, indicate the supporting turn or quotation. Separate what is directly supported from what is provisional. Do not write a final conclusion. Tell me where I should return to the evidence before drafting.
Local analytic ecosystem

Specialized methods generate different maps. ISARI helps researchers think across them.

Classification, affect, relationships, time, synchronous interaction, and place are complementary analytical layers. ISARI provides local interpretive orchestration and project continuity without claiming to replace any of them.

Closing claim

“Less” is not a celebration of weaker research. It is a deliberate allocation of work: the model expands possibilities; the researcher constructs, verifies, and owns the argument.

Five takeaways

1. Power is task-relative

The strongest model is not automatically the most appropriate research instrument.

2. Limitation can protect agency

Provisional output invites interpretation rather than replacing it.

3. Locality supports stewardship

Privacy becomes part of the architecture of analysis and writing.

4. Evidence must remain inspectable

Every retained claim should have a visible path back to source material.

5. The researcher remains the author

AI can stimulate scholarship; responsibility and contribution remain human.

Technical Notes

Running ISARI, File Support, Model Choices, and Workshop Limits

ISARI is a local, portable AI workspace designed to support research workflows and integration with SENA, LACOID, MDCOR, and related outputs.

Launching on macOS

  1. Open the ISARI folder.
  2. Double-click ISARI.app.
  3. If macOS blocks the app, remove quarantine attributes from the folder and relaunch. See the macOS Troubleshooting tab.

Launching on Windows

  1. Open the extracted ISARI folder.
  2. Double-click ISARI.bat.
  3. If Windows shows a warning, run only if the facilitator has confirmed the package source.

Save conversations before stopping ISARI. Unsaved chat content may be lost when the local server shuts down.
